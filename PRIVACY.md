# Privacy Policy for WebMixer

**Last Updated: August 2026**

WebMixer ("the Extension") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our data collection practices.

## 1. Data Collection
WebMixer **does not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, user data, analytics, or browsing history. 

## 2. Local Storage
The Extension uses your browser's local storage (`chrome.storage`) strictly to save your personal preferences (such as MIDI button mappings and your local track queue). This data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to any external servers or third parties.

## 3. Permissions
The Extension requests access to YouTube (`https://www.youtube.com/*`) solely for the purpose of controlling audio playback and applying audio effects locally in your browser. No data about the videos you watch or interact with is collected or monitored by the Extension developer.

## 4. Contact
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the GitHub repository issues page.
